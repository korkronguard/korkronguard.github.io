The Charter

Blood and Honor

◆

The Third War is over, but peace is an illusion. Warchief Thrall has carved out a home for the Horde in the harsh red dust of Durotar, but survival is a daily struggle. To protect this fragile new nation from internal corruption, external threats, and the encroaching Alliance, Thrall established an elite force of enforcers and shock troops, and placed them under the command of High Overlord Varok Saurfang.

We are that force.

We are the veterans. We are the survivors of the First, Second, and Third Wars. We have seen the demonic blood-curse tear our people apart, and we have fought too hard for this new home to see it lost to stupidity, ego, or lack of discipline.

Kor'kron Planner

Plan your talents on the web: Forever's real talent trees, Solo, Dungeon and PvP builds for every spec with the order to learn them while leveling, and a link to share your build in Discord. Pick your gear and the Stat Weights tab shows what a point of each stat is worth to your character, and how far you are from a cap like hit. Its What's New page lists every item each game update adds, changes or removes. Nothing to install.

Open the Planner →

Kor'kron Planner addon

The Planner in your game: follow one of the Planner's builds (or your own, from a link) and see which talent to take next and why, your talent trees next to the build's, what to press, and the best gear for your level, with the next-best choices and Wowhead links to find each piece. Item tooltips tell you whether gear is an upgrade for your build, by how much overall and why, stat by stat, a green arrow marks the upgrades in your bags (Baganator and EllesmereUI too), hovering the gear you wear shows what it gives you, and the Formulas tab shows how your numbers are worked out, where every number comes from, and what each point of a stat does for you. The Stats tab lists what a point of each stat is worth to you. Warriors also get a DPS tab: your damage a second from your own stats, talents and weapons, in each stance, against normal mobs and elites; their stat worths, tooltips and bag arrows are worked out from the gear they wear, with how many points are left before a cap like hit. Other classes will follow. Every spec has a Solo, Dungeon and PvP build, with points you can place your own way, and pressing N draws your build right on the game's own talent window, with the next talent glowing. Type /kp to open it, and /kp export to bring your character's real stats into the Planner. It also shares your character's (and pet's) stats with the Guard and checks the Planner's talent and spell numbers against the game by itself (never chat, whispers or where you are) so the Planner's numbers match Forever exactly. Names never appear on the site, and one command turns sharing off.

Get the Planner addon →
Easiest install, with automatic updates: get the free WowUp addon manager, choose Install from URL, paste the addon's link from above, click Import, then Install on the card that appears. WowUp keeps it up to date from then on.

One Horde, One Front

Addons the Whole Guard Runs

Not made by us: these are other Forever players' world PvP addons that the Guard uses together. They work best when every one of us has them, so install both.

War Effort

Every member becomes a scout: enemy groups you spot are shared with the guild and the Horde and shown live on the map, with an alert when they come close. Scout ranks, daily contracts and a guild leaderboard, and it earns bonus points in Overlord Forever. Open world only, and only PvP-flagged enemies count. Made by PoutineItalienne.

Get War Effort on CurseForge →

Overlord Forever

World PvP objectives: capture territory across seven fronts toward the enemy capital, and hold open-world keeps and outposts as a guild. PvP groups, a faction-wide call to arms, weekly rankings and an Overlord Rank that stays with you. Made by Troma.

Get Overlord Forever on CurseForge →
Installing these two: they live on CurseForge, so the WowUp steps above don't apply. Get the free CurseForge app, choose World of Warcraft and the Forever version, then search for each addon by name; the app keeps them up to date. Or download each one from its page and unzip it into your _forever_\Interface\AddOns folder.

Out of Character

Join the Guard

◆
Faction Horde Game WoW: Forever Ruleset PvP

This lore mirrors who we are as players. We are older gamers, veterans of classic MMOs, and working adults. We love the heavy, immersive feel of the Horde, and we don't follow the toxic, ego-driven culture that plagues modern gaming.

When we raid, we raid: we show up focused, cut the chatter during boss pulls, and respect each other's time so we can clear the dungeon and go to bed. When we PvP, we do it to have a laugh and blow off steam. And when we aren't doing either, we just want a mature, drama-free Hearth to hang out in.

If you understand the value of discipline in a raid, patience with your brothers-in-arms, and honor in the community, the Guard has a tabard for you.
