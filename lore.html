The Charter

The Third War is over, but peace is an illusion. Warchief Thrall has carved out a home for the Horde in the harsh red dust of Durotar, but survival is a daily struggle. To protect this fragile new nation from internal corruption, external threats, and the encroaching Alliance, Thrall established an elite force of enforcers and shock troops. He placed them under the command of High Overlord Varok Saurfang.

We are that force.

The CreedBlood and Honor

We are the Kor'kron. We do not answer to rogue warlords, blood-crazed chieftains, or politicians. We answer to the Warchief, and we operate under the strict, unbreakable code of honor forged by High Overlord Saurfang.

Many in the Horde are young, eager to swing an axe and scream their lungs out in the general direction of the Alliance. We are not them. We are the veterans. We are the survivors of the First, Second, and Third Wars. We have seen the demonic blood-curse tear our people apart, and we have fought too hard for this new home to see it lost to stupidity, ego, or lack of discipline.

The Kor'kron do not scream over the din of battle. We do not panic when the line breaks. We assess the threat, we lock shields, and we execute the Warchief's will. We leave the grandstanding to the grunts; we are here to finish the war.

The Three Pillars of the Guard

To manage the vast threats facing Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms, the Guard is divided into three functional deployments. Every member, regardless of their station, is Kor'kron.

The Raid Force

The Vanguard

“The tip of the Warchief's spear.”

Deep within the mountains of Blackrock, beneath the sands of Silithus, and in the frozen plaguelands, world-ending threats gather. The grunts cannot handle these foes. When a target requires absolute precision, the Warmasters deploy the Vanguard. We march with iron discipline. We are the first to take the field and the last to yield ground. We expect our Legionnaires to know their enemy, carry their own supplies, and execute orders without hesitation.

Where the Vanguard marchesThe Molten Core and Blackwing Lair beneath Blackrock Mountain, Onyxia's lair in Dustwallow Marsh, Zul'Gurub, the gates of Ahn'Qiraj, and Naxxramas itself.

Led by the Warmasters

The PvP Contingent

The Outriders

“The shield of the frontier.”

While the Vanguard strikes at the heart of our greatest enemies, the Horde's borders must be held. The Alliance pushes into the Alterac Mountains, harvests our lumber in Ashenvale, and chokes our supply lines in Arathi. The Outriders are our rapid-response cavalry. Commanded by seasoned Blood Guards, this contingent deploys to contested territories to break the Alliance formations, secure resources, and remind the humans whose land they are stepping on.

Where the Outriders rideBeside Drek'Thar's Frostwolves in Alterac Valley, with the Warsong Outriders in Warsong Gulch, with the Defilers in the Arathi Basin, and on every road the Alliance thinks unguarded.

Led by the Blood Guards

The Social & Support Backbone

The Hearth

“The blood of the Horde.”

An army marches on its stomach, and a soldier fights only for what is waiting at home. The Hearth is the soul of the Kor'kron. Made up of Quartermasters, crafters, scouts, and veterans taking a well-earned rest from the frontline, they are the foundation we build upon. Whether it is mentoring new scouts, securing trade lines in the Barrens, or simply sharing stories by the fire in Orgrimmar, the Hearthguards remind us why we fight. You do not need to swing a sword every night to be an essential part of this clan.

Where the Hearth gathersThe inn fire in the Valley of Strength, the forges of the Drag, the road-camps of the Crossroads.

Led by the Hearth-Callers & Quartermasters

Ranks of Command

The officers of the Guard, highest first.

I

High Overlord

Guild Master

Saurfang's exact title. It carries the ultimate authority over the Vanguard, demanding respect and setting the honorable, low-drama tone for the entire command.

Varok Saurfang is the High Overlord, and every Kor'kron answers to him. Ours holds this banner in his name. Two High Overlords in one city is no contradiction to the Kor'kron; one wears the title, the other carries it for him.

II

Warmaster

Raid Officers

In Horde military structure, Warmasters are the seasoned combat veterans who dictate battlefield strategy and call the shots. On the field, a Warmaster's call is final. Argue at the fire afterward, not at the pull.

III

Blood Guard

PvP Officers

A classic Vanilla PvP rank that perfectly fits the commanders who organize the Outriders and lead the charge against the Alliance.

IV

Quartermaster

Banking & Crafting Officers

Every successful military campaign lives and dies by its supply lines. The Quartermaster ensures the Kor'kron is armed, fed, and supplied before taking the field.

V

Hearth-Caller

Social Officers

Organizers for casual dungeon nights, alt-leveling groups, and general morale. The Hearth-Caller gathers the clan around the fire. They are the social glue that keeps the guild feeling like a brotherhood on off-nights when the armor comes off.

Ranks of the Line

The members of the Guard, highest first.

I

Kor'kron Vanguard

Core Raiders

The absolute tip of the spear. These are our elite who have proven they can hold the line, execute mechanics, and show up prepared week after week.

II

Legionnaire

Raid Team

Standard raid team members who fill the roster, know their class, and pull their weight. The reliable, heavy-hitting backbone of the Horde's military. A highly respected combat rank for a proven soldier.

III

Oathbound

Raid & PvP Recruits

New recruits testing for a permanent spot on the raid or PvP rosters. They have sworn a blood oath to the Vanguard but must prove their discipline in the heat of battle before being officially forged into a Legionnaire or an Outrider.

IV

Hearthguard

Casuals & Friends

Players who just want a great environment without the pressure of any scheduled content. The Hearthguards are the respected defenders of the guild's culture and home. An army is nothing without the people it fights for.

V

Scout

New Arrivals

The entry-level rank for brand-new invites testing the waters to see if the guild is a good fit. Scouts ride ahead, testing the perimeter, getting the lay of the land before officially integrating into the clan.

The Saurfang Code

“The blood made us monsters once. Never again. Not for power, not for vengeance, not for anything.”

No law is carved for the Kor'kron in stone. Saurfang's code is carried in the mouths of the veterans who heard it, and it is taught at the fire to every Scout who rides in.

  1. The Warchief's will is our will.

    We do not serve chieftains, warlords, or the loudest voice in the Valley of Strength. We serve the Warchief, through the High Overlord, through the officer before us.

    Officers make the call. Disagree in whispers or in officer chat, not in raid chat.

  2. Discipline over fury.

    The blood-fury is what the demons gave us. Discipline is what we took back. A Kor'kron who loses himself to rage has handed the demons a victory they did not earn.

    Keep your head. No tantrums, no rage-quits, no flame wars.

  3. The line holds.

    Shields lock. No Kor'kron is left on the field while a brother still stands. We are the first to take the field and the last to yield ground.

    Stay for the whole raid you signed up for. Rez, help, and carry your share.

  4. Silence at the pull.

    Grunts howl. The Kor'kron listen. When the Warmaster speaks, the line is silent.

    Cut the chatter during boss pulls. Save the jokes for the corpse run.

  5. Carry your own supplies.

    A soldier who marches without rations, whetstones and bandages is a burden on the soldier beside him.

    Bring your own consumables, repairs and reagents. Know your fights before you arrive.

  6. Fight the warrior, not the farmer.

    Honor is found in battle with those who can answer the blade. Glory taken from the weak is no glory at all, and the Alliance will remember who slaughtered their peasants.

    Fight good fights. How hard the Outriders hit in world PvP is the Blood Guards' call.

  7. The Hearth is sacred.

    The soldier at the front and the crafter at the forge wear the same tabard. Both are Kor'kron. Neither is lesser.

    Casuals and raiders are equals here. Nobody gets looked down on for how they play.

  8. Never again the blood.

    No Kor'kron will drink of a demon's gift, bargain with the Burning Legion, or take power that must be paid for with their soul. The Guard counts warlocks among its ranks, and they are watched, and they know it. Their power answers to the Warchief, never the other way around.

    The RP flavor behind why warlocks get a hard look in character. All classes are welcome.

Chronicle of the Guard

As it is told at the Kor'kron fire, by the old ones, to every Scout who asks why the Guard does what it does.

Chapter IThe Red Skies of Draenor

Before there was a Horde, there were the clans: Blackrock and Warsong, Frostwolf and Thunderlord, Shattered Hand and Bleeding Hollow, and a dozen more, each with its own fires and its own songs. They hunted the talbuk and the clefthoof beneath a green sky, and they fought one another for honor, and that was the way of things.

Then came Ner'zhul's visions, and Gul'dan's whispering, and the warlocks' fel fire. Draenor's rivers ran thin. The grass turned to red dust. And at the Hold of the chieftains, Gul'dan offered a cup filled with the blood of the pit lord Mannoroth. The chieftains drank, and the fury of the demons ran through the clans like a fever. Our skin turned green with the taint of it. The draenei were butchered in their cities. The world itself was bled dry.

The old ones who remember do not tell this part with pride. They tell it so that it is never forgotten.

Chapter IIThe First War

Through the Dark Portal the Horde came into Azeroth, out of the Black Morass, into a land of rivers and forests the orcs had never dreamed of. The Kingdom of Azeroth stood against us, and it fell. Stormwind burned. The Horde was victorious, and in its victory it tore itself apart: Blackhand slain by Orgrim Doomhammer, Gul'dan's Shadow Council broken, the clans at one another's throats.

Chapter IIIThe Second War

Doomhammer forged the Horde anew and marched north against the Alliance of Lordaeron. Dragons of the Red Flight, enslaved by the Dragonmaw, burned the skies. The forests of Quel'Thalas burned. The capital of Lordaeron itself was besieged. But Gul'dan betrayed the Warchief at the hour of victory, and the Horde was broken at Blackrock Spire. Doomhammer was taken in chains. The Dark Portal was destroyed.

Those who would not surrender fled into the mountain with Blackhand's sons. They are still there. They call themselves the Dark Horde, and Warchief Rend Blackhand sits on a throne in Upper Blackrock Spire as if he were the true heir of the orcish people.

Chapter IVThe Camps

For the rest, there were the internment camps. Walls of stone, guards of Lordaeron, and a sickness no healer could name: a dead, grey lethargy as the demon's blood burned out of us. Warriors who had broken the walls of Stormwind sat in the mud and stared at nothing.

The young ones ask how proud orcs could live like that for years. The old ones don't answer. They remember.

Chapter VThe Son of Durotan

Out of Durnholde came a young orc raised as a slave and a gladiator, with a name his masters gave him: Thrall. He sought out the Frostwolves in Alterac, and old Drek'Thar taught him the way of the shaman: the elements, the spirits of the ancestors, the old way that came before the warlocks. With Grom Hellscream and the Warsong, with Orgrim Doomhammer himself returned from hiding, Thrall broke open the camps one by one.

Doomhammer fell in that campaign, struck down by a knight's lance. Before he died, he gave the son of Durotan his black plate armor and his great hammer, and named him Warchief.

Chapter VIThe Third War

A prophet came to Thrall in a dream and told him to sail west, away from a land that would soon be lost to the plague. The Horde stole ships and crossed the Great Sea. The storms broke our fleet on the shores of Kalimdor.

In that new land, the orcs found allies: the tauren of Cairne Bloodhoof, hunted by the centaur, whom Thrall's warriors escorted to safety in the grasslands of Mulgore. In Ashenvale, Grom Hellscream and the Warsong drank of the blood once more, and fell, and slew the demigod Cenarius. Thrall and Grom faced Mannoroth together in the canyon, and Grom drove his axe Gorehowl into the pit lord's chest and died freeing his people from the curse forever.

At Mount Hyjal, the Horde stood beside the humans of Jaina Proudmoore and the night elves of Tyrande Whisperwind and Malfurion Stormrage, enemies made brothers for one long night. Archimonde the Defiler fell at the roots of the World Tree, and the Burning Legion's invasion was broken.

Chapter VIIDurotar

The Horde claimed the red land and named it Durotar, for Thrall's father. They raised a city and named it Orgrimmar, for the Warchief who fell. And then Admiral Daelin Proudmoore came out of the west with the fleets of Kul Tiras, to finish the war his daughter had ended. He died at Theramore, and the peace between Thrall and Lady Jaina held, though it is thin as a blade's edge.

The Kul Tirans still hold Tiragarde Keep on Durotar's coast and Northwatch Hold in the Barrens. They are not finished with us.

Chapter VIIIThe Guard Is Raised

Peace, the old ones say, is only a war that has not started yet.

The Horde is young. Its grunts were born in the camps, or in the years after, and they have never seen what the blood did to us. The Burning Blade cult chants in the caves beneath Durotar and in the deep tunnels of Ragefire Chasm beneath Orgrimmar itself. The Grimtotem whisper against Cairne in Thunder Bluff. Ambitious warlords dream of being the next Blackhand.

So the Warchief named an elite: the Kor'kron, sworn to the Warchief alone, under the command of High Overlord Varok Saurfang, a Blackrock veteran of every war the orcs have fought, who carries the shame of what the blood made him do on Draenor as if it were a second suit of armor. The Kor'kron Elite stand beside the Warchief's throne in Grommash Hold. The Kor'kron Guard rides wherever the Warchief needs an axe that will not break.

Who We Are

Out of Character

This lore directly mirrors who we are as players. We are older gamers, veterans of classic MMOs, and working adults. We built this guild because we love the heavy, immersive feel of the Horde, and we don't follow the toxic, ego-driven culture that plagues modern gaming.

When we raid, we raid: we show up focused, cut the chatter during boss pulls, and respect each other's time so we can clear the dungeon and go to bed. When we PvP, we do it to have a laugh and blow off steam. And when we aren't doing either, we just want a mature, drama-free Hearth to hang out in.

If you understand the value of discipline in a raid, patience with your brothers-in-arms, and honor in the community, the Guard has a tabard for you.
