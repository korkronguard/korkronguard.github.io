The Guard

◆

Every Kor'kron, by rank. Every Kor'kron tells their own story; choose a name to read their record.

Want your story on the wall? Send an officer:
  1. a screenshot of your character (in game, Print Screen saves one to your WoW Screenshots folder),
  2. your character's name, race and class,
  3. their story, in your own words: where they come from, why they wear the tabard. A paragraph is plenty; a saga is welcome. It goes up exactly as you write it.
